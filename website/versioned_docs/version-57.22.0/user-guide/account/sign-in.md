# Sign in

## What it does

Signing in opens your account on this device so you can see your todos, chats, and files.

## How to use it

1. On **Welcome Back**, enter your **Email** and **Password**.
2. Tap **Log In**.

You land on **Todos**. To sign in with another account provider, tap
**Continue with Google**, **Continue with GitHub**, or **Continue with Apple** and finish
signing in with that provider.

To sign out, open the **Profile** tab and tap **Logout** under **Session**.

## Intended use

Use the same method you signed up with. If you forgot your password, tap
**Forgot password?** on **Welcome Back**; see [Reset your password](reset-password.md).
If sign-in fails, the screen shows **Sign in failed. Please try again.**; check your
email and password, then try again.
