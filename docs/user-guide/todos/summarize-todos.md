# Summarize your todos and rate the summary

## What it does

**Summarize my todos** writes a short summary of your todo list. You can rate the summary,
say what was wrong or right with it, keep it, dismiss it, or copy it.

## How to use it

1. On **My Todos**, add at least one todo.
2. Under **Summarize my todos**, tap **Summarize**. The summary appears under the button.
3. To rate it, tap **Thumbs up** or **Thumbs down** under the summary. Tap the same thumb again
   to take your rating back.
4. After you rate it, a box appears. Type what was good or wrong, for example "It missed a todo",
   and tap **Send**. **Thanks for the feedback** confirms it was sent.
5. Tap **Keep** if the summary is useful, **Dismiss** to hide it, or **Copy** to copy its text.

Tap **Summarize** again for a new summary; your rating starts fresh.

## Intended use

Use the summary for a quick overview of what is on your list. Your ratings, comments, and what
you do with a summary help the team improve how summaries are written. Do not put passwords or
private details in a comment. A summary can be wrong, so check your list for anything important.
