# Create an account

## What it does

Creating an account gives you your own todos, chats, files, and settings. You can sign up
with an email address and password, or with a Google, GitHub, or Apple account.

## How to use it

1. On **Welcome Back**, tap **Need an account? Sign Up**.
2. On **Create Account**, enter your **Name**, **Email**, and **Password**. The password
   requirements under the field turn on as you meet them.
3. Tap **Sign Up**.

You land on **Todos**, signed in. To use another account provider instead, tap
**Continue with Google**, **Continue with GitHub**, or **Continue with Apple** on
**Create Account** and finish signing in with that provider.

## Intended use

Create one account per person. If you already have an account, tap
**Already have an account? Log in** instead. After signing up, confirm your email address
so you can recover the account later; see [Confirm your email address](verify-email.md).
