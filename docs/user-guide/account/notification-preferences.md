# Choose how you get notifications

## What it does

Notification preferences decide which channels the app uses to reach you: the in-app
inbox, email, push notifications, and text messages.

## How to use it

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. Under **Session**, tap **Notification preferences**.
3. Under **Notification preferences**, turn each channel on or off: **In-app inbox**,
   **Email**, **Push**, and **SMS**.
4. Tap **Back** when you are done. Changes save as you make them.

## Intended use

Turn off a channel you do not want to hear from. Every channel starts on. **Email** and
**SMS** only send when your account has an address or phone number for them, and **Push**
only reaches devices where you are signed in to the mobile app.
