# Chat with the AI assistant

## What it does

The **AI** tab is a chat with an AI assistant. It keeps each conversation under **Chats**
so you can come back to it later.

## How to use it

1. Open the **AI** tab.
2. Type in **Type a message...** and tap **Send**. The answer appears as it is written.
3. To start a fresh conversation, tap **New chat** (the plus button next to **Chats**).
4. To return to an earlier conversation, tap it under **Chats**.

To rename a chat, edit its title in the list and save it. To delete one, use its delete
button in the list. To rate an answer, tap **Thumbs up** or **Thumbs down** under it, and
tap **Copy message** to copy its text. When the attach button shows next to the message
box, use it to add files to your message.

## Intended use

Use the assistant for questions, drafting, and working with your todos and files. When it
needs a choice or confirmation from you, it shows a card in the chat; answer it to let the
assistant continue. Answers can be wrong, so check anything important. Do not share
passwords or other secrets in a chat.
