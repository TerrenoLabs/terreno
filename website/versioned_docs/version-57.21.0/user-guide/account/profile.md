# Update your profile

## What it does

The **Profile** tab shows your account details and lets you change your name, email,
password, and how the app looks.

## How to use it

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. Under **Account Details**, tap the edit control next to **Name**, **Email**, or
   **New Password**.
3. Enter the new value and tap **Save**. Tap **Cancel** to keep the old value.

When you change **Email**, also enter your **Current password** in the field that appears.
**Profile updated successfully!** confirms the change.

To switch the app's colors, turn **Dark mode** on or off under **Appearance**.

## Intended use

Use **Profile** to keep your own account details current. Leave **New Password** blank to
keep your current password. After changing your email, confirm the new address; see
[Confirm your email address](verify-email.md).
