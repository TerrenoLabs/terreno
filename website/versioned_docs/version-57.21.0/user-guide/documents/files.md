# Store and organize files

## What it does

The **Documents** tab holds your files in folders. Its **Consents** section lists the
consent forms you have agreed to.

## How to use it

1. Open the **Documents** tab. **Files** is selected at the top.
2. To add a file, tap **Upload file** and choose it.
3. To make a folder, tap **New folder**, enter a **Folder Name**, and tap **Create**.
4. Tap a folder to open it.
5. To remove a file, tap **Delete** on its row. On the web, tap **Download** on a file's
   row to save a copy.

To review the consent forms you signed, tap **Consents** at the top.

## Intended use

Use **Files** for your own documents. **Upload file** only shows when uploads are turned
on for your account, and **Download** is available on the web. Deleting a file removes it
for good.
