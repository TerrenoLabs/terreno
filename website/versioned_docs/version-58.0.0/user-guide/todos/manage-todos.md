# Add, complete, rename, and delete todos

## What it does

**My Todos** keeps your personal list of things to do. Open todos appear under **To Do**,
and finished ones move to **Completed**. A count under the title shows how many remain.

## How to use it

1. Open the **Todos** tab.
2. Type in **New Todo** and tap **Add Todo**. The todo appears at the top of **To Do**.
3. Tap the checkbox next to a todo to mark it done. Tap it again to move it back to
   **To Do**.
4. To rename a todo, tap its title or the edit button next to it, type the new title, and
   press Enter or tap away from the field.
5. To delete a todo, tap the trash button next to it.

## Intended use

Use todos for your own tasks; other people cannot see them. Changes show at once, even
without a connection, and sync when the device is back online. A deleted todo cannot be
restored.
