---
category: Deprecated
---

- The `UserModel` type alias (use `PassportUserModel` or `TerrenoUserModel`), `createRouterWithAuth`, `setupEnvironment`, `gooseRestRouter` and `GooseRESTOptions` are deprecated and are removed in 59.0.0 or three minors after this release, whichever is later.
- Omitting `TerrenoApp`'s `legacyAuth` option is deprecated: set it explicitly. The default becomes `false` in Terreno 60.
